FAQ

Before you request a quote.

Quick answers to the questions that usually come up before a mobile detail.

Do you come to my location?

Yes. Curbside Detailing offers mobile detailing so the service can be done at a convenient location for you.

How much does detailing cost?

Pricing depends on the vehicle, its current condition, and what you want done. Send us a quote request and we’ll give you a price based on the actual job.

What services can I request?

You can request interior detailing, exterior detailing, a complete interior and exterior detail, or describe something specific that you want addressed.

What information should I include?

Include your vehicle year, make and model, vehicle type, general condition, the service you’re interested in, and any stains, odors, pet hair or other areas that need extra attention.

Do you need access to water or electricity?

Requirements can vary by service and location. If access to water or electricity is needed for your job, we’ll confirm that with you before the appointment.

What happens if it rains?

Weather can affect mobile exterior work. If conditions are not suitable for the service, we’ll contact you to adjust the timing or plan.

How long does a detail take?

Timing depends on the vehicle size, condition and requested work. Once we review your quote request, we can give you a better idea of how long the job should take.

What about pet hair, stains or odors?

Include them in your quote request and, when possible, add photos. Extra-condition work is considered when we review and quote the job.

Do I need to be home during the detail?

Not necessarily, as long as vehicle access and any required instructions are arranged in advance. We can coordinate the details when the appointment is confirmed.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Lower Mainland, BC. Enter your city or area in the quote form and we’ll confirm whether we can travel to your location.

Can I send photos with my quote request?

Yes. Photos are recommended because they help us see stains, pet hair, heavy dirt or anything else that may affect the work and quote.

How should I prepare my vehicle?

If possible, remove valuables and personal items before the appointment so the areas being detailed are easy to access.
