FAQ

Before you book.

Do you come to my location?

Yes. Curbside Customs offers mobile detailing so the service can be done at a convenient location for you.

How much is a full detail?

Full interior and exterior details are $250 for small vehicles, $290 for regular coupes, sedans and hatchbacks, and $350 for trucks, vans and full-size SUVs.

What is included in the full detail?

The current package includes a hand wash and paint decontamination, full interior vacuum and steam clean, stain and odor attention, leather and plastic UV protection, plus wheel, tire and trim cleaning and dressing.

What information should I include when booking?

Include your vehicle year, make and model along with anything unusual you want us to know about, such as stains, odors or specific areas that need extra attention.
