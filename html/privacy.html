Privacy

Clear about the information you send us.

This page explains what information Curbside Customs receives through the website, why we use it, and the third-party services involved in receiving your messages and quote requests.

Plain-language summary

We use the information you submit to answer questions, prepare detailing quotes, coordinate service and keep basic business records. We do not use the website to sell submitted personal information.

Last updated: August 30, 2026.

1. Information you may provide

When you use the quote or contact forms, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your city or service area.
  • Vehicle year, make, model, type and condition.
  • The detailing services you are interested in.
  • Preferred dates, notes and other information you choose to include.
  • Vehicle photos uploaded with a quote request.

2. How we use submitted information

Information submitted through the website is used to respond to you, review the work your vehicle may need, prepare a quote, coordinate scheduling or service details, and maintain reasonable business records.

3. Form processing

Our website currently uses FormSubmit to deliver quote requests and contact-form submissions to our business email address. Information you submit through those forms is transmitted through that service before it reaches us.

You should avoid sending passwords, financial account information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through the website forms.

4. Website hosting and technical information

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, timestamps or request logs as part of operating and securing their services.

5. Vehicle photos

Photos submitted with a quote request are used to understand vehicle condition and help prepare an accurate quote. Please avoid including unrelated personal documents, licence details or other sensitive information in uploaded images.

6. How long information is kept

We keep submitted information only as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, provide or coordinate service, maintain basic business records, resolve issues, or meet applicable legal obligations.

7. External links

The website may link to services such as Google and Instagram. Those services have their own privacy practices and policies, which apply when you visit them.

8. Your questions or requests

If you have a privacy question or want to ask about information you previously submitted, contact Curbside Customs at contact.curbside@gmail.com or (604) 562-5372.

9. Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website, forms or third-party services change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
