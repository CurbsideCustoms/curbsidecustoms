About Curbside Detailing

Built by people who actually care about cars.

We started Curbside Detailing with a simple idea: detailing should feel personal, convenient and worth the money. No rushed handoff. No one-size-fits-all approach. Just careful work from people who enjoy bringing vehicles back to life.

Our Story

Cars first. Business second.

We’re two car enthusiasts with a shared interest in vehicles, detailing and automotive repair.

Hands-on automotive training and repair experience gave us a deeper appreciation for the little things that separate a rushed job from careful work. That same mindset carries into every vehicle we detail.

Curbside Detailing was created to give people a more personal option for vehicle care. Whether someone wants their daily driver cleaned up, is getting a car ready to sell, or simply wants to enjoy their vehicle again, the goal is the same: make it look and feel noticeably better.

Why We Do It

That feeling when a car looks right again.

A proper detail changes how a vehicle feels to own. Paint looks deeper, the interior feels fresh, and the small things you stopped noticing suddenly stand out again.

That transformation is the part we enjoy most — and it’s why we built Curbside Detailing around quality work instead of volume.
